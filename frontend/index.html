Frequently Answered Questions

How does client-side de-identification work in browser RAM?

Before any radiograph payload leaves your computer, an in-memory WebAssembly module parses the binary DICOM dataset. It strips Tag (0010,0010) Patient Name, Tag (0010,0020) Medical Record Number, and burns zero-intensity black padding over four-corner pixel burn-in regions. Raw identifiers never touch external networks.

What happens when the model encounters non-upper extremity images?

Verifact incorporates an anatomical scope rejection gate. Lower extremity or axial skeletal radiographs trigger an immediate out-of-scope advisory flag, redirecting the study to standard unassisted triage.

Can attending radiologists override the AI findings?

Yes. The attending physician maintains complete editorial authority. Radiologists can edit the drafted text, recalibrate calipers, reject false-positive bounding boxes, and stamp their electronic signature.

How are signed reports delivered to hospital PACS and EHR?

Completed studies export as HL7 FHIR DiagnosticReport payloads and cryptographically sealed PDF documents stamped with the physician's license number and audit hash.
