What Verifact Is

ASSISTIVE AI

Verifact is a human-in-the-loop clinical decision support co-pilot engineered specifically for high-acuity emergency radiology, urgent care centers, and trauma triage suites.

Screening & Heatmaps

Pre-screens upper-extremity projections (shoulder, humerus, elbow, forearm, wrist, hand) and generates class activation gradient heatmaps showing suspicious cortical disruptions.

Structured Drafts

Generates draft RadLex-compatible clinical findings (cortical breach morphology, displacement degree, anatomical landmark) ready for the physician’s immediate critique or revision.

Fatigue Protection

Engineered to mitigate diagnostic misses during 2:00 AM – 6:00 AM nocturnal shifts where diagnostic fatigue rises by 24%, without removing or diluting attending doctor authority.

02

What It Is NOT (Safety & Intended Use)

REGULATORY BOUNDARIES
Statutory Intended Use Scope

Verifact operates exclusively as an adjunct decision-support instrument. Under institutional protocol and medical device guidelines, the software is strictly subject to the following non-negotiable operating constraints:

Not Autonomous

Never diagnoses independently. It does not prescribe interventions, clear patients for discharge, or release reports directly to patient charts.

Not for Axial / Pediatric

Not validated for pediatric open physes (growth plates) or the axial skeleton (cervical spine, thoracic spine, lumbar spine, pelvis, ribs, skull).

Not a Board Substitute

Cannot replace formal board-certified radiologic interpretation. Every bounding box and observation requires credentialed electronic sign-off.

Known Limitations & Edge Cases

FOUR FAILURE MODES

In our retrospective stress testing, model reliability degraded predictably under four specific radiographic conditions. Clinicians should exhibit elevated scrutiny when reviewing:

A False Positive Risk

Pediatric Open Growth Plates

Normal cartilaginous physis lines (distal radial and ulnar physes) present radiolucent bands that mimic acute cortical discontinuities. The model frequently misinterprets physiological growth lines as Salter-Harris fractures.

Mitigation: Enforce age threshold (>18 yrs) before running automated segmentation.
B Degraded Activation

Motion Blur & Poor Exposure

Underexposed films or patient motion artifact diminish edge gradient sharpness. This low signal-to-noise ratio suppresses Grad-CAM activation maps below the 0.65 threshold, causing genuine hairline fractures to be overlooked.

Mitigation: Radiologic technologist repeat series requested for non-diagnostic exposures.
C High Contrast Artifact

Orthopedic Hardware In-Situ

Metallic fixation plates, cortical screws, and intramedullary rods introduce harsh beam-hardening boundaries. Convolutional kernels regularly highlight screw holes or plate margins as cortical fracture lines.

Mitigation: Manual exclusion toggle on views containing surgical fixation implants.
D Partial Undercounting

Comminuted & Fragmented Injuries

While the model reliably bounds the primary cortical disruption in severe trauma, it frequently consolidates multiple small comminuted micro-fragments into a single broad zone, missing secondary free bone chips.

Mitigation: Recommend supplementary non-contrast CT for complex articular reconstruction.

Privacy & Client-Side Architecture

ZERO CLOUD PHI

Local-Memory DICOM Sanitation Pipeline

Patient health information never leaves your clinical workstation session.

HIPAA • DICOM PS 3.15 Compliant
Edge-First Anonymization

DICOM Tag Group 0010 (Patient Name, MRN, Date of Birth, Institution ID) is zeroed out directly inside browser WebAssembly RAM before any pixel buffers are passed to the neural network inference engine.

Outer-Margin Burn-In Masking

The outer 10% perimeter of the pixel array is automatically analyzed and sanitized, eliminating burnt-in clinic overlays, department timestamps, and equipment serial numbers from downstream activations.

Zero Remote PHI Storage

No patient identifiers, uncompressed DICOM records, or clinical notes are persistently logged to external servers. The neural weights are delivered to the edge; data stays behind the hospital firewall.
